# Public Experience + Trust Boundary V1

**Consumption does not require identity. Authority does.**

Public LifeOS and public Space begin as `GUEST`; public media, TV, Radio, News and Digipedia never trigger TrustID or passkey. `IDENTIFIED` means a TrustID-backed session. `STEP_UP_VERIFIED` is reserved for sensitive policy-controlled operations. App/Space presentation is independent of trust state.

Studio remains TrustID, relationship, and server-capability authorized. Browser state, installation, a public URL, or Space assets never grant Studio authority. Guest Space state is namespaced as `space.lifeos.guest`; identified state remains namespaced to its TrustID. Offline cached public content may be consumed when available; no remote identity, payment, or calling capability is invented.
