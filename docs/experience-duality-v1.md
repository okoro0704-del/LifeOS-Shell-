# Experience Duality V1

LifeOS presentation is explicit: `APP` is its conventional UI-first shell; `SPACE` is its opt-in Space Contract presentation. App command chrome and wake/command overlays do not mount in Space mode, and Space controls do not mount in App mode.

Installing/opening a web app does not grant Space capabilities. Product routes and session remain App-owned; Space snapshots stay namespaced by Space identity. TV, radio and calling are future Space capability ownership targets; this phase does not extract them.
